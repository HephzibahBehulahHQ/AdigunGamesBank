The commissioning order

How to run this experiment
  1. Choose the capacitor bank and both resistors. Use the plan meter to check energy, current and timing.
  2. Start charging. Choose Capture & hold while voltage and stored energy are both inside the required windows.
  3. Pass the leakage hold, then discharge below the release voltage within the current, power and time limits.

Pause at any time. While paused, advance the same circuit by 0.1 s to make precise keyboard or touch decisions. A completed hold pauses automatically before the discharge test.

The RC model and its limits

The model integrates the exponential voltage response exactly for each switch position. Charging includes the parallel leakage resistor; holding discharges through leakage; release discharges through the bleed resistor and leakage in parallel. Energy is ½CV². Values use volts, amperes, ohms, farads, seconds and joules; the interface also shows µF, mA and mJ.

Components are ideal. Temperature, tolerances, equivalent series resistance, dielectric absorption and real failure transients are omitted. Current and power limits stop the game immediately. A pause freezes simulated time, including the automatic pause after a passed hold. Reset starts a new virtual experiment at zero charge. This is a learning game, not a procedure for real energized equipment.
