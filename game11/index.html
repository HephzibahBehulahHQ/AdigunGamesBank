Rules, access and development status

This is an original 11-a-side football game with fictional players and kits. A complete ball crossing the goal line inside the frame scores. Throw-ins, corners and goal kicks restart automatically; time stops while paused or during a restart. Offside, fouls, cards, substitutions, league management and online multiplayer are not implemented.

The campaign contains 10,000 deterministic match configurations. AI speed, decision time, pressure, shot accuracy and match objectives change with level. Configuration tests do not mean all 10,000 matches have been manually played. Progress is saved on this device after a qualifying full-time result.

The pitch uses real WebGL geometry and the quality readout shows its actual drawing buffer. 4K requires compatible hardware; production visual quality, full accessibility and hardware performance remain under evaluation. Keyboard and touch controls, text match events, visible focus and reduced camera motion are implemented. Spatial play still relies on seeing the pitch.
