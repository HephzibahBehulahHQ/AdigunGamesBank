Rules, access and development status

This original football game has 22 starting players, a tactical opponent, ball physics and goalkeeper saves. Whole-ball scoring, offside involvement, contact fouls, direct/indirect free kicks, penalties and yellow/red cards affect play. Offside is judged when a teammate kicks, then penalised on a flagged player’s touch or challenge; direct throw-ins, corners and goal kicks are exempt. Centre-point positions and contact-speed thresholds are simulation simplifications. Advantage, handball, detailed visual obstruction, extra time, league management and online multiplayer are not implemented; this is not full IFAB-law compliance.

At a free kick use Pass or Shoot; at a throw-in use Pass; at a penalty choose the shot target and Shoot. Movement waits until the restart is taken. The opponent restarts automatically; your taker plays after four idle seconds if you have not acted. Indirect restarts need a second player’s touch to score. Time stops during setup and while awaiting the kick. Queue up to five real roster replacements across three stoppages; sent-off players cannot be replaced. Two yellows or a direct red remove the player; fewer than seven players abandons the match without a campaign award.

The campaign contains 10,000 deterministic match configurations. AI speed, decision time, pressure, shot accuracy and match objectives change with level. Configuration tests do not mean all 10,000 matches have been manually played. Progress is saved on this device after a qualifying full-time result.

The pitch uses real WebGL geometry and the quality readout shows its actual drawing buffer. 4K requires compatible hardware; production visual quality, full accessibility and hardware performance remain under evaluation. Keyboard and touch controls, text match events, visible focus and reduced camera motion are implemented. Spatial play still relies on seeing the pitch.
