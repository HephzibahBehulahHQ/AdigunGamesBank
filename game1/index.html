Keyboard & touch

Tap a signal to pulse it. On the 3D scene, use arrow keys to select a signal and Enter to pulse. Space pauses. U undoes. All controls also work with Tab, Enter and Space.

No timer or rapid reactions are needed. Numbers and text identify every signal as well as colour.

About this workshop

An original signal-routing puzzle. Dark, Amber and Cyan are abstract logic states, not electrical voltages or AC phases. The board grows from 3 × 3 to 6 × 6 across five difficulty tiers. Its 10,000 deterministic configurations are computationally checked for reachable solutions; this is not a claim of 10,000 manual playtests.
