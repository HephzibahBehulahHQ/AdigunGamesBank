Model, controls and learning references

This is a playable ideal DC simulation: 3–15 V sources, ohmic resistors, ideal wires, switch contacts and an instantaneous fuse limit. All bench resistors use a 5 W model. Temperature, component tolerance, transients and manufacturer fuse curves are outside this model. The 25% fuse margin is a game constraint. This game does not provide instructions for real mains wiring or certify physical circuits.

Levels use deterministic circuit challenges and five increasing network/logic tiers. Automated validation can establish generated solvability; it does not mean 10,000 levels have been manually playtested. English interface; complete German, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin localization remains in progress.
