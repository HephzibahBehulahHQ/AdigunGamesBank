    How the network model works

    Every wire tile is one conductive node. North connects to south, east to west, and both tiles must present a matching terminal. Diagonals and row edges never connect. All specified lamps must receive the feed without exposed live branches, an unwanted auxiliary load, or a path straight to return. The rotation budget applies to this job; failed jobs can be retried.

    This virtual panel models ideal wires and resistive loads with prewired returns. Load current uses I = V / R; branch currents add at the supply. A direct ideal short trips the virtual fuse without inventing a finite short-circuit current. Wire resistance, component tolerances, fuse time-current curves and mains installations are outside this game.

    Technical references: OpenStax: Ohm’s law and Kirchhoff’s rules. Layouts and commissioning objectives are original game design.
