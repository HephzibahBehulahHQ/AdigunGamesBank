Setting and source

Courage in the valley

In 1 Samuel 17, David chooses five smooth stones and confronts Goliath with a sling. The armies face each other across the valley of Elah.

The practice shields, measured wind and non-lethal bronze-shield objective are original gameplay. They are not additional claims about Scripture.

Adaptation, physics and release notes

This is the first Bible Chronicles chapter, not the entire Genesis-to-Revelation story. Original procedural development art depicts the valley, sling, shields and figures; no photorealistic or archaeological reconstruction claim is made. The encounter ends with a shield hit and contains no graphic injury.

The game uses gravity at 9.81 m/s², a constant sideways acceleration to represent wind, a level collision floor and exact target-plane intersection. Air resistance and real sling technique are simplified. Ten thousand deterministic configurations change range, crosswind, offset and target size; they are challenges within this one game.

The 4K setting requests a 3840 × 2160 drawing buffer. The rendering readout reports the actual buffer. Performance depends on the device; choose Auto if needed. Current production qualification and browser evidence are recorded in the project status.
