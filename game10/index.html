Track rules and accessibility

Cross twelve gates in order on every lap. A finish-line crossing alone earns nothing. Leaving the tarmac adds three seconds and invalidates the current sector. Recover to your last clean gate to continue; recovery adds five more seconds. Run out of time and the race ends. Retry keeps previously completed races.

The racing model uses metres, seconds, acceleration, braking and a tyre-grip limit. These are original arcade cars and circuits, not manufacturer data or driver training. Changing driving mode pauses the run. Leaving the page pauses it too. Reduced motion uses a fixed circuit camera; step driving removes the need for timed reactions. Live telemetry and the engineer guide provide an alternative to reading the scene.
