    Word model, parity and mission rules

    Unsigned words range from 0 to 2ⁿ − 1. Signed words use two’s complement: the highest bit contributes −2ⁿ⁻¹ and the other bits keep their positive weights. Signed mode changes the decimal interpretation; shifts remain logical and insert zero.

    Even parity makes the total number of ones even; odd parity makes it odd. The receiver checks both the requested value and parity. A rejection consumes an operation and a fault allowance, without advancing the frame. Exhausting either budget loses the mission. All accepted frames must arrive in order. Restart repeats the same mission and preserves previously completed missions.

    This is an exact mathematical word model with a manually advanced clock. It does not model propagation delay, voltage thresholds, metastability or a specific integrated circuit. Sources: MIT: integer encodings and error detection; Nexperia: shift and storage registers; Texas Instruments: odd/even parity.
