    Time after the kick before you commit. An early dive reaches the corner sooner.

    The keeper commits to the advertised side. Crosswind, available power and ball flight change your placement. A post or save ends that attempt.

    Make the space count.

    1. Read which side the keeper is loading. Left and right always mean the view from the penalty spot, including your goalkeeper turns.
    2. Take five shots using aim, elevation and power. Use placement shortcuts as starting points; compensate for wind on later challenges.
    3. Take the gloves for five opposition shots. Read the striker, choose your dive position, height and delay, then select Face penalty.
    4. Finish with more goals than the opposition and meet the goal target to unlock the next challenge. A draw, defeat or missed target can be retried.

    With the pitch focused: ←→ aim or dive position, ↑↓ shot elevation or dive height, W/S shot power or dive delay, Space take the attempt, Esc pause, N next kick, R retry. Every action also has a visible button or slider.

    Training rules and simulation limits

    This is an original ten-kick training campaign: five shots followed by five goalkeeper turns. A draw requires a retry, with no sudden death. The opening is 7.32 × 2.44 m; the spot is 11 m away. Gravity affects airborne shots, crosswind is a stated lateral acceleration, and grass slows rolling balls. There is no spin, contested rebound, encroachment decision or live AI service.

    Study pace slows playback, not the trajectory or scoring. Keyboard controls work without WebGL. English is implemented; full German, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin translations remain in progress.
