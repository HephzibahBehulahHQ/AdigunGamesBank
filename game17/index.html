    PLACEMENT SHORTCUTS · ADJUST AFTER SELECTING

    The keeper commits to the advertised side. Crosswind, available power and ball flight change your placement. A post or save ends that attempt.

    Make the space count.

    1. Read which side the keeper is loading. Left and right always mean your view from the penalty spot.
    2. Set aim, elevation and power. Use the shortcuts as starting points; compensate for wind on later challenges.
    3. Shoot and watch the result. Select Next kick between penalties. Reach the goal target after all five kicks to unlock the next challenge.

    With the pitch focused: ←→ aim, ↑↓ elevation, W/S power, Space shoot, Esc pause, N next kick, R retry. Every action also has a visible button or slider.

    Training rules and simulation limits

    This is a five-kick training campaign against an original local goalkeeper, with a goal target rather than two-team sudden death. The opening is 7.32 × 2.44 m; the spot is 11 m away. Gravity affects airborne shots, crosswind is a stated lateral acceleration, and grass slows rolling balls. There is no spin, contested rebound, encroachment decision or live AI service.

    Study pace slows playback, not the trajectory or scoring. Keyboard controls work without WebGL. English is implemented; full German, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin translations remain in progress.
