How Goal Line works

Every round has five striker kicks. All five must finish. Reach the round’s goal target to unlock one new level; later tiers ask for four, then five goals. There are 10,000 levels, ending with Professional Master.

This is a striker training drill. The goalkeeper patrols across the goal. A run-up feint draws a delayed commitment toward the zone you had selected. Study the keeper’s stage and position, then choose where and when to shoot. An early shot can still be tracked; a committed keeper has already chosen a side.

Study mode is the default. “Advance time” moves the keeper by exactly 0.10 seconds, and advances ball flight by the same amount after a strike. Turn real time on for continuous play. Pause and switching tabs both stop all simulation time.

Nine zones choose launch angles, not guaranteed destinations. Power changes the arc and arrival time; the crosswind on each kick changes horizontal flight. Shots can be saved by actual keeper contact, hit the frame, or miss.

All outcomes and progression use the same simulation with or without the 3D view. 4K UHD requests an actual 3840 × 2160 rendering buffer when your device supports it.
