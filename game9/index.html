Drill rules

Commit to a clearance once the ball is within 2.3 m of the line. An early kick meets the rolling ball 0.45 m from the line. Be within 0.62 m laterally at contact. Every ball must fully cross its gate 8 m upfield to unlock the next drill.

From Apprentice onward, a pressing player can intercept the clearance. Read their position and adjust your aim to get around them without missing the gate.

From Skilled onward, some feeds bounce. Wait for a high ball to drop into the defender's reach before clearing it.

From Expert onward, the receiving teammate moves along the outlet line. The outlet reading predicts the receiver's position when the ball arrives, once a clearance is available.

Ground resistance slows the ball. A stronger clearance reaches the outlet sooner, changing when it passes the opponent. A weak clearance can stop before the outlet.

With the drill controls focused during live play, hold A or D to move, Q or E to aim, Z or X for kick speed, and press F to clear. Escape pauses the feed. On touch, tap a direction or use Hold to stop. Use Tab and Enter for the buttons and arrow keys on the sliders. Negative angles aim left.

On a standard-mapped controller, use the left stick to move during live play, D-pad left or right to aim, the bumpers for kick speed, and the bottom face button to clear. Use Menu to start or pause, the right face button for the next ball or drill, and the top face button to restart.
