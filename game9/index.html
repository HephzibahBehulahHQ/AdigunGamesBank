Drill rules

Make contact within 2.3 m of the line and within 0.62 m laterally. A clearance counts when the entire ball crosses the gate 8 m upfield. Every ball must be cleared to unlock the next drill.

Use Tab and Enter for the buttons and arrow keys on the angle slider. All readings are in metres. Negative angles aim left.
